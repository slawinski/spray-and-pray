# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Solo job-seeker managing their application pipeline. Uses this tool at their desk, often in focused multi-hour sessions. Context: high-stakes career moves, needs reliability and speed.

## Product Purpose

Spray and Pray (SAP) tracks job applications, generates AI-tailored CVs, runs mock interviews with LLM evaluation, and produces deep-dive learning content from interview weaknesses. Success = more interview readiness, fewer ghosted applications, faster CV turnaround.

## Brand Personality

**Dark, focused, precise.** Terminal-native seriousness. No decoration for decoration's sake. Every element earns its pixels. Feels like a professional tool, not a consumer app. Confident restraint.

## Anti-references

- No SaaS cream/sand/pastel backgrounds
- No bubbly rounded cards with soft shadows
- No gradient text or glassmorphism
- No "delightful" startup microcopy
- No hand-drawn sketch illustrations

## Design Principles

1. **Earn every pixel** — nothing decorative survives unless it serves the task
2. **Dark-first, not dark-only** — dark theme is native, not a toggle
3. **Terminal confidence** — the TUI and web surfaces share the same DNA
4. **Speed is a feature** — loaders and transitions must feel fast, not just be fast
5. **Show the work** — reveal what the LLM is doing, don't hide it behind spinners

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast minimums
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Keyboard navigable (TUI is inherently keyboard-driven)
